Fish stands for **f**riendly **i**nteractive **sh**ell. Unlike other shells (zsh or bash), fish has built-in [syntax highlighting](https://fishshell.com/docs/current/interactive.html#syntax-highlighting), [autosuggestions](https://fishshell.com/docs/current/interactive.html#autosuggestions), and [tab completion](https://fishshell.com/docs/current/interactive.html#tab-completion) with a shallow learning curve.
